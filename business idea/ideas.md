List 2: 9 Detailed Platform Concepts
1. AI life manager
   One app that plans your day, reminds you to eat, move, rest, and prioritise tasks based on your energy, not just deadlines.

2. Digital burnout recovery platform
   A system that helps people reset from screen fatigue, overconsumption, and online overload without deleting their entire life.

3. Personal brand operating system
   A tool that helps creators manage content ideas, posting, analytics, deals, and income in one place without juggling five apps.

4. Mental health for high performers
   Therapy and support designed specifically for entrepreneurs and creators who don’t relate to traditional mental health spaces.

5. AI powered skill matcher
   A platform that looks at your interests and skills and suggests income paths or businesses you can realistically start.

6. Privacy focused social network
   A space where people can share thoughts and content without algorithms pushing anxiety or comparison.

7. Health tracking beyond fitness
   An app that tracks sleep, stress, digestion, and habits together instead of just steps and calories.

8. Community based learning platforms
   Courses paired with real accountability, mentors, and peers instead of one time videos you never finish.

9. Decision making assistant for adults
   An AI that helps you think through life decisions logically when emotions take over.
